# Disclaimer

This repository documents my personal, self-directed study toward the **Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP)** certification.

## Authorised use only
Every technique, tool, and command shown here is practised exclusively in:
- **Isolated virtual lab environments** that I own and control, or
- **Legal training platforms** that grant explicit authorisation to attack their targets — TryHackMe, Hack The Box, and OffSec Proving Grounds.

Nothing in this repository is intended to encourage, assist, or be used for unauthorised access to any system. **Attacking systems you do not own or have written permission to test is illegal** in Malaysia (Computer Crimes Act 1997) and virtually everywhere else, and can end a security career before it starts.

## No exam content
In keeping with OffSec's confidentiality policy, **no OSCP exam machines, flags, or specifics are published here.** Writeups cover only retired or practice targets on platforms that permit them.

## Educational purpose
The material here reflects my own learning and is provided for educational purposes. I accept no responsibility for how others choose to use it. Use knowledge responsibly and lawfully.
